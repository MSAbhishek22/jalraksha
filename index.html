Problem · Solution · Outcome

The problem

Pumps break often, and in the monsoon flood water can get into open wells and make the water unsafe. But no one in the village knows, at any one time, which water points are working and which are not. A family can walk to a pump, find it broken, then walk to a well that may not be safe. The panchayat does not know which broken pump matters most, so repairs can happen late and in the wrong order.

The solution

JalRaksha is one shared board for the village’s water points — hand pumps, wells, tap stands and the RO plant — each with a clear status: working, broken, or not safe to drink. Anyone can update a point with one tap, or press the voice button and speak in Hindi. One Flood Mode switch marks open, low-lying points as “check before drinking” and brings the safe points to the top.

The outcome

The village can see where its working water is, in normal weeks and in flood weeks. The panchayat gets a repair list ranked by how many families use each point, so the pump that serves the most families is fixed first. This board is a demo for one pilot village, Rampur, and its sample data is labelled as demo data.

How to make water safe · पानी सुरक्षित कैसे करें

1. Boil it · उबालें पीने के पानी को उबालें। उबाल आने के बाद एक मिनट और उबालें, फिर ठंडा करके साफ़, ढके हुए बर्तन में रखें।
Boil drinking water. After it starts boiling, keep it boiling for one more minute. Let it cool, and keep it in a clean pot with a lid.
2. Chlorine tablet · क्लोरीन की गोली अगर उबाल न सकें, तो पैकेट पर लिखे हिसाब से क्लोरीन की गोली मिलाएँ और आधा घंटा रुककर पानी पिएँ।
If you cannot boil, add a chlorine tablet as written on the packet, wait 30 minutes, then drink.
3. During floods · बाढ़ के समय बाढ़ के समय खुले कुएँ और तालाब का पानी बिना जाँचे न पिएँ। ऊपर SAFE लिखे स्थानों से पानी लें।
During floods, do not drink from open wells or the pond without checking first. Take water from the points marked SAFE above.
